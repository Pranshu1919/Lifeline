Please Do Not Report Fake Disaster Cases

Municipal emergency rescue teams, boats, ambulances, and fire units are scarce, life-saving assets. Submitting false, test, or prank distress reports wastes critical responder time and delays rescue for real victims facing life-or-death situations.

False emergency calls are subject to legal prosecution under disaster management laws.
